The OpenRail Association greatly depends on a vibrant community of contributors, developers, and enthusiasts. We welcome anyone interested in contributing to the OpenRail ecosystem, whether through code, feedback, networking, or other forms of support.

Depending on your areas of interest and capabilities, there are several ways to get involved.

### Community Events {#events}

We regularly hosts events and participates in conferences and fairs to bring together projects, experts, and interested parties. These events provide the perfect opportunity to get to know the community, learn about the latest developments, and contribute to the OpenRail ecosystem.

View all events on the [web calendar]({{< event-links "web" >}}) or download the [iCalendar/ICS file]({{< event-links "ics" >}}).

### Mailing Lists and Social Media {#channels}

You are welcome to join the OpenRail mailing list for general discussions and announcements. This is the best way to stay informed about updates.

To sign up, please visit the [OpenRail mailing list page](https://lists.openrailassociation.org/postorius/lists/general.lists.openrailassociation.org/) and enter your email address. You will receive a confirmation email to complete the subscription process.

You will also find us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/openrailassociation/), [Mastodon](https://floss.social/@openrail), and [GitHub](https://github.com/OpenRailAssociation). Follow us on these platforms to stay updated on recent developments.

### Join as a member {#membership}

We invite companies and organizations to become members of the OpenRail Association, alongside the [current OpenRail members]({{% relref "/#members" %}}) who have agreed on the association’s principles and realize the benefits of an Open Source organisation for the railway sector.
